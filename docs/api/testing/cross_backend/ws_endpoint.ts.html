testing/cross_backend/ws_endpoint.ts

Where a spawned backend's WebSocket endpoint is, and how the cross suites open a socket on it as one account's session.

The socket-driving cross suites take WsEndpointTarget as their endpoint options (flat, or nested as ws on a suite whose socket cases are optional), and open a socket on a fixture account's session through open_session_ws, so the cookie read, the missing-cookie assertion and the transport choice are written once. Sockets on another credential — a daemon token, a bearer, a session a case logged in on its own transport — and the raw-socket cases open their own way.

(target: WsEndpointTarget, account: WsSessionAccount, options: OpenSessionWsOptions): Promise<WsClient> import {open_session_ws} from '@fuzdev/fuz_app/testing/cross_backend/ws_endpoint.js';

Open a real-upgrade socket on target as account's session: its session cookie on the upgrade, target.origin (or base_url) as the Origin.

The cookie is the static one account.create_session_headers carries, not whatever fixture.transport's jar holds now, so a case that rotates the session through fixture.transport first upgrades on the stale cookie.
