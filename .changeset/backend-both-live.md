- feat: `testing/cross_backend/backend_both_live.ts`: `describe_backend_both_live_tests({orders, database_url, empty_database, offer_role?, gated_ws_path?})` (`BackendBothLiveOrder`, `BackendBothLiveTestOptions`) runs, per order over an emptied database, the first backend's bootstrap and the second's boot over it, then both at once: a session logged out and one revoked by id, an API token revoked, a password changed and an account deleted on the second are refused on the first at once, each credential proven live on both first, and an offer the first made and the second accepted confers on the first; each revoke's changed tables are compared by content digest after both drain, and each test ends with both backends' invariant check. The per-process limits are pinned by name (`docs/security.md` § Single-Process Architecture), so a cross-process close or notification that lands within the watch window fails them: a socket on the first outlives the second's password change, logout and token revoke and keeps answering per-message calls; a role-gated socket outlives the second's role revoke and keeps receiving the account's pushes, while a per-message call needing the role is refused; an offer made on either notifies no socket on the other; and a second backend booted before the first's bootstrap keeps advertising bootstrap on `/status` until it restarts or a valid bootstrap attempt on it is refused by the database's lock, which closes its window, and resolves no keeper for its daemon token until it restarts. The configs must share what the swap's do.
