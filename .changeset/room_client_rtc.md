feat: the frontend side of rooms, and WebRTC over them — a room client, a peer with perfect negotiation, and a full-mesh call, every side effect injected

- `actions/room_client.svelte.ts`: `RoomClient` (`new RoomClient(rpc, {acting?, log?, set_timeout?, clear_timeout?})`) keeps a reactive `RoomState` per room (`client.room(room_id)`) — `members`, `seat`, `seat_status`, `watching`, `ended`, `watch_ended`, `error`, `local_state` — over the WS-only `room_*` verbs. `join`, `leave`, `watch`, `unwatch`, and `set_state` record a want that one runner per room converges on, one request at a time; `send` relays at once. Every verb runs untracked, so an `$effect` may call one. Listeners are isolated: one that throws is logged and the rest still hear. Events before a join's or watch's answer are buffered and replayed past its `seq`, a live room drops events at or below its last `seq`, and a message reaching a watch while the join is in flight is held for the seat. A seat lost to a disconnect is rejoined on the next connection with `rejoin_of` (a `room_seat_moved` refusal ends it as moved, a `rate_limited` one is retried after its `retry_after`, up to `ROOM_REJOIN_RATE_LIMITED_RETRIES_MAX` times); one lost to `room_seat_lost` is not. The client follows the connection with its own effect (`sync_connection()` stays public), `receive(method, params)` takes the pushed `room_*` notifications (`is_room_notification_method`), `add_listener` subscribes a `RoomListener`, and `dispose()` leaves every seat and drops every watch. `create_room_rpc(socket)` builds its `RoomRpc` straight over a `FrontendWebsocketClient`'s `request`, never through the RPC client's HTTP fallback, its answers read forward-compatibly
- `rtc/rtc_signal.ts`: the signal envelope a room payload carries — `{"rtc": 1, "description": {type, sdp}}` or `{"rtc": 1, "candidate": RTCIceCandidateInit}` (`RTC_SIGNAL_VERSION`, `encode_rtc_signal`, `decode_rtc_signal`, which answers `not_rtc_signal` for a room's other payloads)
- `rtc/rtc_peer.svelte.ts`: `RtcPeer`, one `RTCPeerConnection` to one remote member over `RtcPeerDeps` (`create_peer_connection`, timers; `create_rtc_peer_deps` for the browser), with perfect negotiation (`rtc_peer_is_polite`: the lower member id yields), candidates queued until the remote description is set (those arriving while an offer is ignored fail silently), ICE restarted on failure and after a lasting disconnect (`RTC_ICE_DISCONNECTED_RESTART_MS`), and fixed transceiver slots (`RtcSlot`, `RTC_VOICE_SLOTS`) so `set_track` is a `replaceTrack` with no renegotiation (the slot list is protocol: changing it bumps `RTC_SIGNAL_VERSION`); remote media is `remote_tracks` by slot. It takes a `send(signal)` function and an `on_signal` entry, never a transport
- `rtc/rtc_mesh.svelte.ts`: `RtcMesh`, a full-mesh call over an `RtcMeshRoom` (`RoomState` is one) — a peer per other member while seated, closed when a member leaves or the seat ends, the local tracks fanned out to every peer (`set_track`), each peer's signals sent one at a time, ICE servers a list or a function of the seat (for credentials the join answer's `extra` carries), messages addressed to another member dropped, and any topology but `mesh`, or a peer that can't be made, reported in `error`
