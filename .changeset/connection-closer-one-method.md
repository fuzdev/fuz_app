break: `ConnectionCloser` has one close method — `close_connections(target: ConnectionCloseTarget)` — and `close_all_connections()` for shutdown, as the Rust spine's `ConnectionCloser`; `close_sockets_for_session` / `_token` / `_account` / `_role` and `close_all_sockets` are removed (`ConnectionRegistry`, `create_sse_connection_closer`, `noop_connection_closer`, and `RealtimeCloser` alike), and the free `close_connections(closer, target)` is now the method. `ConnectionCloseTarget`'s session variant is `{kind: 'session', session_hash}` (was `session_token_hash`), and `ConnectionIdentity.token_hash` is `session_hash`. New: `to_connection_close_target(event)` (`actions/connection_closer.ts`, the audit row → target mapping the revocation guard uses, twin of the Rust `ConnectionCloseTarget::from_audit_event`) and `connection_identity_matches(identity, target)` (`actions/connection_registry.ts`, the per-connection match, twin of the Rust `ConnectionIdentity::matches`). Testing: `RecordedClose` is `ConnectionCloseTarget | {kind: 'all'}` — `create_recording_closer` records each target as given, so `{method: 'session', id}` is `{kind: 'session', session_hash}`, `{method: 'token', id}` is `{kind: 'token', api_token_id}`, `{method: 'account', id}` is `{kind: 'account', account_id}`, `{method: 'role', id, role}` is `{kind: 'role', account_id, role}`, and `{method: 'all', id: null}` is `{kind: 'all'}` — and `assert_close_call(call, expected)` takes the expected record. To migrate: a hand-written closer implements the two methods (switch on `target.kind`); `registry.close_sockets_for_account(id)` is `registry.close_connections({kind: 'account', account_id: id})`, `close_sockets_for_session(hash)` is `close_connections({kind: 'session', session_hash: hash})`, `close_sockets_for_token(id)` is `close_connections({kind: 'token', api_token_id: id})`, `close_sockets_for_role(id, role)` is `close_connections({kind: 'role', account_id: id, role})`, and `close_all_sockets()` is `close_all_connections()`; read `identity.session_hash` where you read `identity.token_hash`; the free `close_connections(closer, target)` is `closer.close_connections(target)`; `assert_close_call(call, 'session', hash)` is `assert_close_call(call, {kind: 'session', session_hash: hash})`
