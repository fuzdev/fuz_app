break: `apply_route_specs` and `compile_action_registry` (so `create_rpc_endpoint` and `register_action_ws`) throw at registration on a schema slot holding neither a Zod nor a `@fuzdev/fuz_schema` schema — a route's `params`, `query`, `input`, `output` or an `errors` entry, an action's `input` or `output` — naming the spec and the slot (`Route "POST /api/things": output is neither a Zod schema nor a @fuzdev/fuz_schema schema`); `schema_to_surface` throws the same refusal naming its `source`, so in `create_app_server` a slot surface generation reads first fails boot that way. Before, such a value in an `input` or a `query` mostly threw a `TypeError` naming nothing, and the rest mounted, failing at request time or never; `create_app_server` refused most at boot as a JSON Schema it could not write, but booted a route whose `null` `params` left its params unvalidated — that now throws at registration and at boot. An absent `params`, `query` or `errors` entry stays allowed, and an `errors` entry left `undefined`, a route's or a middleware's, now reads as absent: it leaves the derived or middleware schema for its status in place (`strip_undefined_error_schemas`), where before it erased that schema from the merge and surface generation refused it. `merge_error_schemas` returns `Record<number, ValidationSchema> | null` and `DerivedErrorSchemas` is `Record<number, z.ZodType>`, neither holding `undefined`. The check is the new `assert_validation_schema` (`schema_issues.ts`). To migrate: put a schema in the slot the throw names — `z.null()` or fuz_schema `literal(null)` for a route with no body.
