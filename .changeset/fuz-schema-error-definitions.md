break: the REST error schemas (`http/error_schemas.ts`) are `@fuzdev/fuz_schema` schemas, not Zod, built from per-code REST error definitions: `rest_error(status, code, fields?, headers?)` makes a `RestErrorDef` of one `ERROR_*` code at one HTTP status, its body `loose_object({error: literal(code), ...fields})` as `.schema`, and every code a route or middleware answers has one, named `REST_*` after its code (`REST_TABLE_NOT_FOUND`, `REST_RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` with `retry_after` and a `Retry-After` `headers`, `REST_INSUFFICIENT_PERMISSIONS` with `required_roles`, `REST_AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED_BOOTSTRAP` for `/api/account/status`'s 401 with `bootstrap_available`, …); `to_error_schemas(defs)` groups definitions into a status map. `ApiError`, `PermissionError`, `CredentialTypeRequiredError`, `TokenScopeRequiredError`, `RateLimitError`, `PayloadTooLargeError`, `UnsupportedContentTypeError`, `ForeignKeyError`, `ActorRequiredError`, `ActorNotOnAccountError`, `NoActorsOnAccountError` and `AccountVanishedError` keep their names and are fuz_schema `loose_object`s, each single-code one its definition's schema, and the role and credential-type lists are typed `Array<string>` where Zod's `.readonly()` typed them `ReadonlyArray<string>`. Renamed, with nothing left at the old name: `ValidationError` → `InvalidInputError`, `RouteErrorSchemas` → `ErrorSchemas`; `RateLimitKey` moves to `rate_limiter.ts`, a fuz_schema `one_of`; `READY_ERROR` (`db/schema_ready.ts`) is gone, its codes now `ERROR_SCHEMA_DRIFT` and `ERROR_DB_UNREACHABLE` beside the new `ERROR_INTERNAL_ERROR`. The Zod `RouteAuth` schema (`http/auth_shape.ts`) is deleted: `RouteAuth` is an interface of the same shape, its invariants checked by `assert_route_auth_invariants` as before, and `AuthAxisState` is a fuz_schema `one_of`. `ActionManifestEntry` and `ActionManifest` (`testing/cross_backend/action_manifest.ts`) are fuz_schema `object`s. Every verdict and output is Zod's, and the surface is unchanged but for each `readOnly` annotation, now written after its node's `type` and `items`; regenerate a committed surface snapshot that holds a role or credential-type 403. To migrate: import `InvalidInputError`, `ErrorSchemas`, and `RateLimitKey` (from `rate_limiter.ts`) under their new names, parse these schemas with fuz_schema's `parse` / `is` / `parse_or_throw` or `parse_any` (`parse_or_throw(InvalidInputError, body)` for `ValidationError.parse(body)`), hold them in a fuz_schema schema, as a Zod object can no longer hold them, and type an auth shape with the `RouteAuth` interface where a `RouteAuth.parse` checked one.
