break: the `{acting}` route queries are `@fuzdev/fuz_schema` schemas, not Zod: the `query` of `GET /health`, `GET /tables` and `DELETE /tables/:name/rows/:id` (`create_db_route_specs`), of the bare-hash `GET /api/facts/:hash` (`create_serve_fact_route_spec`) and of `GET /api/surface` (`create_surface_route_spec`), and `AuditStreamQuery` (`auth/audit_log_route_schema.ts`, `GET /audit/stream`), are `object({acting: ActingActor})` over the fuz_schema `ActingActor` twin, refusing unknown keys as the `z.strictObject`s did, `acting` a UUID in either case in and lowercase out; `AuditStreamQuery`'s type, read by `Infer`, keeps its name and shape, and the acting check still finds every one, so authorization is unchanged. Only the `query` slot moved: these routes' `input`, `output` and `errors` stay Zod, as does the query of `GET /tables/:name`, whose paging params are a Zod preprocess. Their surface entries are unchanged as JSON (fuz_schema writes the `acting` node's `description` last, where Zod wrote it first; a committed surface snapshot written through `gro gen` changes by that move, so regenerate it), as are their production `400 invalid_query_params` bodies; under DEV a bad query's `issues` keep their `kind`, `path`, `expected` and `received`, each `message` now fuz_schema's text (`acting: expected uuid, received string`) in place of Zod's; a handler's `get_route_query(c)` on these routes is Hono's null-prototype query record itself when no entry changed it (a plain copy when `acting` was lowercased), where Zod always returned a plain copy. To migrate: code calling `.parse` / `.safeParse` / `.shape` / `.extend` on `AuditStreamQuery` (or these specs' `.query` as Zod) uses fuz_schema's `parse` and `def` (`@fuzdev/fuz_schema/schema.ts`; a spread of `AuditStreamQuery.def` into `object` in place of `.extend`), or `parse_any` (`schema_issues.ts`) for either library.
