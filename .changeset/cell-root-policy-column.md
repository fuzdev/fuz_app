break: a root's contribution policy moves out of `data` into a manage-tier `cell.policy` column (`JSONB`, nullable, a table CHECK holding it to roots and to JSON objects), since `cell_update` let an editor-grant holder rewrite `data.policy` and turn moderation off for a space. The policy is opaque to the spine: `CellCreateAuthorize` is now an object — `authorize(auth, input)`, whose input carries `root_policy` (the governing root's column) in place of `root_data`, and an optional `validate_policy(policy)` that accepts or refuses every non-null policy write; with no authorizer, or one without `validate_policy`, a policy write is 400 `cell_policy_unsupported`. A root takes its policy at `cell_create` (new `policy` input; beside a `parent_id` it is 400 `cell_policy_root_only`) and changes it through the new manage-tier `cell_policy_set` verb `{cell_id, policy}` (`null` clears) — the 404 mask, then 403 `cell_policy_manage_only` for a viewer who doesn't manage the root (an editor grant included), then `cell_policy_root_only`, then validation — audited as the new `cell_policy_set` event with the policy written and the one it replaced (`CellPolicySetAuditMetadata`, in `cell_audit_events`). `CellJson` gains `policy` (readable by any viewer), `CellRow` / `CELL_COLUMNS` gain it after `moderation`, and a clone is born without one. New: `CellPolicy`, `cell_policy_set_action_spec`, `query_cell_set_policy`, `ERROR_CELL_POLICY_ROOT_ONLY` / `_UNSUPPORTED` / `_MANAGE_ONLY` / `_INVALID` (the conventional reason a `validate_policy` refuses with). `cell_grant_create` takes an optional `authorize_grant_create` (`CellGrantCreateAuthorize`, on `create_cell_grant_actions` and `create_all_cell_actions`), asked of a manager after the spine's gates and before the write, a level change included; `false` is 403 `ERROR_CELL_GRANT_NOT_AUTHORIZED` with no grant and no audit row. The `fuz_cell` migration is rewritten in place. To migrate: drop and re-bootstrap your cell tables (a database with live cell data — visiones — exports and re-imports it, moving each root's `data.policy` into its `policy`), since the `full_cell_schema` migration already applied won't re-run; turn a function `authorize_create` into `{authorize, validate_policy}`, reading `input.root_policy[kind]` where it read `input.root_data.policy[kind]`; set a root's policy through `cell_create`'s `policy` or `cell_policy_set` — `data.policy` is no longer read by anything; add `policy: null` to any hand-built `CellJson` fixture, since `CellJson` now requires it; regenerate any committed schema fixture (`expected_schema.json`), and the generated action codegen and attack-surface snapshots, which gain the `cell_policy_set` method; and read `TEST_SPACE_POLICY` as the policy map itself — it no longer wraps it in `{policy: …}`
