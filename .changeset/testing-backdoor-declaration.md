break: backends declare their `_testing_*` backdoors as `capabilities.testing_backdoors` (from the new `TESTING_BACKDOOR_METHODS`), with no family default, so a config fails typecheck until it states the list; `describe_testing_backdoor_cross_tests` now takes `capabilities`, checks the declaration (an undeclared known backdoor must answer `method_not_found`, a declared one must be mounted), fires its credential rows at the declared ones plus `extra_methods` (a consumer's own daemon-token seams), and can't be excluded, by suite or by case, nor handed another `capabilities.testing_backdoors` through `overrides`; `describe_spine_cross_suites` requires `_testing_reset` + `_testing_drain_effects` (and `_testing_put_fact` with `fact_serving`), skips `credential_deadline` / `origin_conformance` / `ws_outbound_overflow` with a reason naming the backdoor when it isn't declared, refuses an exclude or override of a suite that skips itself, and gains `exclude_cases` — `{[suite]: {[describe > test name path]: reason}}`, each test skipped at run time with its reason, an entry naming no collected test failing the run. To migrate: add `testing_backdoors: [...]` listing what your test binary mounts to each config's `capabilities`, drop `exclude` entries for `testing_backdoor` and for suites you excluded only for a missing backdoor, and narrow a wholesale suite exclude to `exclude_cases` where only some of its tests can't run; a backend whose WS endpoint doesn't serve its backdoors passes `overrides.testing_backdoor: {ws: undefined}`
