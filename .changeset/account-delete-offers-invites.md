break: an account delete ends what was pending for the account — the offers made to it and the invites it issued — and an offer can't be made to a deleted account

- break: `account_delete` supersedes every pending role-grant offer made TO the account (`query_role_grant_offer_supersede_incoming`, reason `account_deleted`, an audit row each), as it already superseded the ones the account made. An offer made before a delete no longer waits it out and becomes acceptable after the undelete: an accept answers 400 `role_grant_offer_terminal`
- break: `query_role_grant_offer_create` refuses a recipient that is not an active account (`RoleGrantOfferRecipientNotActiveError`) and a grantor that is not (`RoleGrantOfferGrantorNotActiveError`), treats a tombstoned actor of a live account as the mismatch, and takes `FOR SHARE` on both accounts in one statement (`query_account_lock_live_many`, id order) before its insert — so it runs inside the caller's transaction. `role_grant_offer_create` answers a deleted or missing recipient as `role_grant_assign` does: 404 `account not found`, or 400 `role_grant_offer_actor_account_mismatch` when an actor is named — a missing account was a 500, a deleted one a 200. A grantor or an invite's issuer deleted mid-request gets 401 `authentication_required` and nothing is created
- break: `account_delete` and `account_purge` delete the unclaimed invites the account issued (`query_invite_delete_unclaimed_by_creators`, an `invite_delete` audit row each); a signup with one answers 403 `no_matching_invite`. Claimed invites are untouched. `invite_create` locks its issuer's account before its insert
- role grants are kept through a delete and come back with the undelete; superseded offers and deleted invites do not. Every supersede cascade returns and audits its rows ordered by `created_at, id`; the last-admin guard locks its rows in account-id order
- testing: `account_lifecycle` and `role_grant_offer_lifecycle` pin the above on both spines; `account_lifecycle` takes an optional `signup_path`. A consumer that calls `query_role_grant_offer_create` directly maps the two new errors; one that mints invites through `query_create_invite` for an issuer takes `query_account_lock_live` on the issuer first
